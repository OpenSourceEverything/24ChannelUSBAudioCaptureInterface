# 008 — Validate the product and complete economics

State: OPEN, G1-B M1/PRD-005. Independent of the generated USB experiment.
Required before production analog/PCB commitment; no commercial inputs are
invented to close this task.

Use [the product-definition worksheet](../research/product-definition.md).
Record buyer/use case, simultaneous input mix, justified 96-kHz/Linux scope,
controls/monitoring needs and differentiation. Preserve the owner's requested
all-24-channel provisional modes until a separate product decision changes them.

Record target selling price/channel and batch volume, then complete BOM,
assembly/yield, test/calibration, enclosure/PSU, freight/fees, warranty/returns
and support/update costs. Compare equivalent simultaneous local analog
channels/rates and total installed customer cost; headline digital I/O is not
an equivalent analog input count.

Exit: evidence-backed scope/price/volume/cost and the owner's production
investment decision. Customer/supplier messages and purchases need their own
authorization. PRD-001–004 release/audio/clock/endurance evidence stays later.
